Pets & Their People: Handy Finds for Life with Pets

Sharing your home with a pet comes with plenty of love, a little mess, and lots of things to keep organized. These product ideas can help make everyday pet routines more convenient.

1. Pet Hair Removers

Pet hair removers can help tackle fur on furniture, clothing, rugs, and car seats.

What to look for: Surface compatibility, reusable or disposable design, ease of cleaning, and handle comfort.

Shopping tip: Check which surfaces the tool is designed for. A remover made for upholstery may not be suitable for every fabric or delicate material.

2. Slow Feeder Bowls

Slow feeder bowls have ridges or sections that encourage pets to work around the bowl to reach their food.

What to look for: Appropriate size, stable base, food-safe materials, ease of cleaning, and a design suited to your pet.

Shopping tip: Choose a size and difficulty level that suits your pet. Check with your veterinarian if you have concerns about your pet's eating habits.

3. Washable Pet Beds

A comfortable pet bed gives your furry friend a place to rest and can help keep their favorite spot tidy.

What to look for: Bed dimensions, washable covers, filling, material, durability, and your pet's sleeping preferences.

Shopping tip: Measure your pet while resting and compare those measurements with the bed's actual sleeping area.

4. Pet Treat Storage

Storage containers can help keep treats organized and easy to reach during training or everyday routines.

What to look for: Secure lids, suitable capacity, easy-to-clean materials, and a design that keeps treats protected.

Shopping tip: Check the container's dimensions and whether it is intended for the type of treats you use.

5. Pet Travel Accessories

Travel accessories such as portable water bowls, carriers, and seat covers can make outings with pets more manageable.

What to look for: Correct sizing, secure closures, easy cleaning, durability, and suitability for your type of travel.

Shopping tip: For car travel, choose properly designed restraints or carriers appropriate for your pet and vehicle. A regular seat cover is not a safety restraint.

6. Interactive Pet Toys

Interactive toys can provide opportunities for play, exploration, and mental stimulation.

What to look for: Pet size, material, durability, cleaning instructions, and age-appropriate design.

Shopping tip: Choose toys suited to your pet's play style and supervise play when appropriate. Replace damaged toys.

Before You Buy:
  • Choose products suited to your pet's size and needs.
  • Check materials and cleaning instructions.
  • Read recent customer reviews, including critical ones.
  • Look for secure, durable designs.
  • Compare prices and check return policies.

Happy Pets, Happier Routines

You don't need every pet gadget on the market. Focus on useful, safe products that fit your pet's routine and make life a little easier for both of you.
