Beauty & Self-Care: Little Things That Feel Like a Treat

Beauty and self-care products can add a little convenience and comfort to everyday routines. The best finds are the ones that suit your needs, fit your budget, and are easy to use.

1. Satin Pillowcases

Satin pillowcases have a smooth surface and can be a comfortable addition to your bedtime routine.

What to look for: Fabric type, pillow size, zipper or envelope closure, washing instructions, and care requirements.

Shopping tip: Satin describes a type of weave, not necessarily a specific fiber. Check whether the pillowcase is polyester satin, silk, or another material before comparing prices.

2. Makeup Brush Sets

A makeup brush set can help organize the tools you use for applying and blending makeup.

What to look for: Brush types, bristle material, handle comfort, included storage, and cleaning instructions.

Shopping tip: Choose a set based on the makeup products you actually use. A larger set isn't automatically a better buy.

3. Cosmetic Organizers

Trays, drawers, and countertop organizers can make it easier to find makeup and skincare products.

What to look for: Available space, compartment sizes, material, ease of cleaning, and whether drawers slide smoothly.

Shopping tip: Measure your counter or vanity before ordering. Clear organizers can make it easier to see what's inside.

4. Reusable Makeup Remover Pads

Washable makeup remover pads are an alternative to disposable cotton rounds for some parts of a skincare routine.

What to look for: Fabric softness, size, number included, washing instructions, and whether a laundry bag is provided.

Shopping tip: Check the care instructions and consider how many pads you'll want available between laundry days.

5. Heated Styling Tools

Curling irons, straighteners, and hot brushes can help create different hairstyles at home.

What to look for: Adjustable temperature settings, tool size, automatic shutoff, voltage compatibility, and safety features.

Shopping tip: Choose a tool that suits your hair styling needs. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and use heat protection when appropriate.

6. Spa Headbands and Wrist Wash Bands

Soft headbands and wrist bands can help keep hair away from your face and catch water during a skincare routine.

What to look for: Comfortable fit, absorbent material, stretch, durability, and washing instructions.

Shopping tip: Look for washable materials that feel comfortable and are easy to keep clean.

Before You Buy:
  • Check materials and care instructions.
  • Compare sizes and included accessories.
  • Read recent customer reviews, including critical ones.
  • Check return policies and warranties where applicable.
  • Choose products that fit your routine and budget.

A Little Reminder

You don't need every beauty trend or gadget. Pick the items that genuinely make your routine more comfortable or convenient.
